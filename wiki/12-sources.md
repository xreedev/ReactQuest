# 12 — Sources & Confidence Ratings

## How to read the ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | First-hand candidate account of a specific interview, or a primary job description from the company |
| **B** | Aggregated platform data or multiple corroborating accounts; self-selected samples |
| **C** | Practitioner commentary or curated guidance; useful, not evidence of frequency |
| **D** | Commercially motivated (resume tools, training providers); directional only, verify before relying |

**Excluded on purpose:** "Top 30 [Company] React interview questions" SEO pages. These are generated content, not evidence, and are the main reason most online prep advice is wrong about this segment.

## Known limits of this research

1. **Frequency is not measured.** Writeups are self-selected. "Commonly asked" here means "appears across several independent accounts", not a percentage.
2. **Some accounts are dated** (2022–2023). React-ecosystem specifics move; the JS-fundamentals core does not.
3. **Company-specific processes change**, and vary by account, city and staffing agency. Treat `01` as a prior, not a guarantee.
4. **No first-hand CV exists in the corpus for an Indian service-company lateral React hire.** The CV guidance in `08`–`09` is assembled from adjacent evidence (shared FAANG-oriented CVs + Indian ATS/portal mechanics + candidates' own résumé tips), not from a shortlisted TCS/Infosys CV.
5. **Vendor sources (rating D)** have a commercial interest in claiming ATS rejection risk. Their structural advice matches independent sources; their specific product and statistic claims do not have primary backing.
